Paperverie

A cozy bilingual story-driven flight adventure from Pearlverie Studios, created for families and designed for touch and controller play.

Proposed Apple Arcade edition, submitted for consideration. Paperverie has not been announced as accepted into Apple Arcade.

The preview PDF describes the proposed Arcade edition. Its no-in-app-purchases wording applies to that proposal. The released App Store edition includes 30 free levels and an optional Full Journey Unlock, currently $4.99 in the US App Store.

Privacy and contact

Paperverie is a Pearlverie Studios project. This page presents a proposed Apple Arcade edition submitted for consideration; it is not an announcement that Paperverie has been accepted into Apple Arcade.

This preview website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub handles website requests under its privacy statement. External destinations handle visits under their own policies.

The linked game privacy policy describes the standard Android, iPhone and iPad editions. It does not establish a privacy policy or contract for the proposed Apple Arcade edition.
